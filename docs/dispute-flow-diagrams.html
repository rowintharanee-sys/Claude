The one thing to remember: in Visa Allocation (fraud and authorization disputes) the decision to escalate to arbitration is yours, and the window is 10 days. In every other scheme flow, after our rebuttal the decision belongs to the issuer — 10 days for Visa Collaboration and Discover, 15 days for Mastercard.

Visa — Allocation (fraud 10.x & authorization 11.x)

Your evidence is filed as the pre-arbitration, so there is no separate rebuttal stage. When the issuer rejects it, the next move is yours and you have only 10 days.

Visa — Collaboration (processing errors 12.x & consumer disputes 13.x)

You get two bites: the dispute response, then the pre-arbitration response. The issuer then has 10 days to escalate or accept.

Mastercard (all reason codes)

The same shape as Visa Collaboration with different vocabulary. Your rebuttal window is the tightest in the Mastercard flow, and the issuer gets 15 days rather than 10 to decide on arbitration.

Discover / Diners Club (all reason codes)

Mirrors Visa Collaboration, including the 10-day issuer escalation window. Discover is often both the network and the issuer.

American Express

A three-party network: Amex is the network and the issuer, so no pre-arbitration or arbitration cycle exists. Everything rides on the inquiry and the representment.
